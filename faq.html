What to know before your first round at Champion Boxing Gym.

Choose a plan on the Memberships page, review the membership acknowledgement and complete your payment securely through Stripe. You can also visit the gym if you want help deciding which training option fits you.

Wear comfortable workout clothing and bring boxing gloves if you have them, hand wraps, a jump rope, water and a towel.
