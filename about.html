Our Legacy

Building Champions. Transforming Lives.

Champion Boxing Gym has operated for more than a decade as a licensed USA Boxing gym, training amateur and professional fighters while serving youth and families across Clayton County and metro Atlanta.

Athletes from the gym have competed in events including Golden Gloves, Silver Gloves, Junior Olympics, Ringside International and Sugar Bert national competition.

Our Mission

Competition matters, but the heart of the gym is helping young people build discipline, emotional control, confidence and practical life skills through boxing.

A Safe Haven

The gym gives youth a constructive place to train, release stress and build positive relationships away from everyday pressures at home, school and in the neighborhood.

Community Focus

Champion Boxing Gym uses education, leadership development, life-skills programming and community partnerships to strengthen the people and neighborhoods around it.

Our Commitment

As a community business in Clayton County, the gym works to keep youth engaged in positive, strength-based activities and support prevention of juvenile crime and violence.
